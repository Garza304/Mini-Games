Custom words

Add inside jokes, team names, server names, whatever you like. One per line or comma-separated. They're mixed into the next game, and the default list fills the rest of the 25 cards.

How to play

  • Split into Red and Blue. Each team picks one spymaster; everyone else is an operative.
  • Spymasters see the secret key: which words belong to which team, the neutral bystanders, and the one assassin.
  • On your turn, the spymaster gives a one-word clue plus a number: how many cards it relates to.
  • Operatives click cards to suggest them (everyone sees the suggestions), then click again to reveal. You get up to number + 1 guesses.
  • Your color: keep going. Neutral or the other team's color: turn over. The assassin: your team loses instantly.
  • The team that starts has 9 agents to find, the other has 8. First team to find all of theirs wins.
  • Clue of 0 or ∞ means unlimited guesses. You can always end your turn early.
